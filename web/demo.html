The login fields are prefilled. Each sign-in starts a fresh demo, with two sample tenants, two stores and four inventory items. Your session lasts one hour.

What to try

  1. View Lagos and Texas stores with their local currencies and addresses.
  2. Create a tenant or store, then add inventory. Inventory inherits the selected store's address and coordinates.
  3. Provision sample users, assign tenant/store roles, configure feature settings and inspect audit records.
  4. Use “Try the sample marketplace” after login to build a basket and simulate test payment or cancellation.

You can edit every currently implemented administration resource inside this demo. Marketplace and payment settings control implemented marketplace checkout. The demo payment simulator stays isolated from real providers; loyalty and offline transactions remain unimplemented. Live payments, purchases, merchant settlements and production records are not accessible.

Use fictional information only. Demo sessions are unavailable after expiry; expired files are removed when the next demo starts. Up to 30 concurrent demo workspaces and 500 administrative edits per workspace are allowed. Sign in again to reset.

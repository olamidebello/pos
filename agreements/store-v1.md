# Store participation agreement
Version: store-v1 | Draft for platform integration

By selecting “I agree” as an authorized representative, you agree on behalf of the identified store to:

1. Provide accurate business identity, physical address, store coordinates and contact information, and maintain authority to operate the store and receive its payments.
2. Maintain accurate product barcodes, shelf labels, prices, taxes, stock and offers. Resolve catalog or shelf-label discrepancies before charging the shopper; an OCR price alone is not proof of product identity or an authorized charge.
3. Disclose the seller, final total, applicable fees and return/refund policy before payment. No unspecified commission or subscription fee is authorized by this agreement; any such fee needs a separate disclosed agreement.
4. Accept payment only through an approved processor and configured merchant account. Never collect raw card details through the platform. Issue a paid receipt only after server-side payment confirmation.
5. Train authorized checkout staff to validate receipt payment status, store, items and prior exit use through the platform. Do not accept a screenshot alone as payment verification. Handle disputed or failed scans through staff assistance.
6. Protect shopper and employee information, restrict staff access to their store and assigned duties, and use location and transaction data only for disclosed operational purposes. Do not sell or use it for unsolicited marketing without a separate lawful basis.
7. Publish and honor applicable returns, refund and support procedures. Address refund and product complaints as the seller; preserve applicable consumer rights.
8. Cooperate in investigating payment errors and unauthorized receipt use. Access may be restricted for misuse, with a support/appeal route disclosed before live activation.

The representative must affirm their authority separately. A downloadable copy and acceptance record must remain available to the store.

Launch prerequisites: identify the platform operator, support contact, merchant settlement terms, privacy notice, retention policy and applicable jurisdiction. This draft does not set arbitration, governing law, liability exclusions or waivers.

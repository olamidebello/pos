# Shopper agreement
Version: shopper-v1 | Draft for platform integration

By selecting “I agree,” you agree to:

1. Confirm the correct store before shopping. You may find stores by address or choose one manually; optional device location helps identify nearby stores.
2. Scan every item and its correct quantity. Confirm product identity when scanning a shelf label or price. Report incorrect prices or missing items to staff rather than entering an unverified price.
3. Review the basket, discounts, taxes and total before finishing. Payment is manual unless you separately enable automatic payment for a saved processor-managed payment method.
4. Pay successfully before leaving with goods. A pending or failed payment does not produce a paid receipt. The platform must confirm payment on its server before showing the receipt as paid.
5. Present the digital receipt or its QR code for staff verification at checkout. Staff may compare purchased items and quantities with the receipt. A receipt cannot be reused for another shopping trip or exit.
6. Keep account credentials private and report unauthorized transactions through the support contact displayed by the platform.

The selected store is the seller and must show its identity, contact details and applicable return/refund policy before purchase. This agreement does not waive consumer rights or authorize unrelated charges.

The privacy notice must explain what account, shopping, payment-reference and location information is used, who receives it, retention and available privacy controls. Device location and camera access are requested only when needed; manual store and barcode entry remain available. Marketing permission is optional and separate.

You may decline this agreement and stop using self-checkout. Staff-assisted checkout availability is determined by the store and must be clearly displayed.

Launch prerequisites: identify the platform operator and support contact and publish the privacy notice. Make the accepted agreement version downloadable from the account.

The original project artwork was made with the built-in image generation tool. No existing brand was supplied as a reference. No trademark clearance or legal exclusivity is claimed. The generated master images are preserved in `web/assets/global-pos-logo.png` (transparent) and `web/assets/global-pos-world.png`. Derived install icons are sized by the project's Sharp image pipeline, with a warm cream background, for browser and home-screen use.

## Final generation prompts

### World storefront illustration

Use case: illustration-story. Create original premium website hero artwork for Global POS, a worldwide location-based shopping and point-of-sale platform. Wide landscape illustration, refined clean isometric editorial 3D style, deep forest green and warm cream with small gold accents. A central smartphone with a simple barcode scan motif, encircled by three different small storefront buildings resting on a subtle curved globe surface; location pins and flowing connecting paths communicate local inventory linked to stores worldwide. Generous breathing space, sophisticated soft shadows, crisp friendly geometry. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no existing brand logos. This is conceptual brand artwork, not a working interface diagram.

### Logo symbol

Use case: logo-brand. Design one original polished brand symbol for Global POS, a platform connecting local stores worldwide through phone shopping and geolocated inventory. Transparent background. A strong simple geometric forest-green location-pin silhouette incorporating an abstract circular globe and tiny storefront awning in its negative space; one restrained golden accent line suggests scan-and-go. Flat vector-like crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup. Centered single icon with generous transparent margins, instantly recognizable at small sizes. No text, no letters, no watermark, no existing brand logo.
